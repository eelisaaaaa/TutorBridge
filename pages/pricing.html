Why are our rates so much lower?

Singapore physical tuition agencies charge significant overhead fees and local tutors demand rates of S$50–S$80/hr due to Singapore's high cost of living.

By connecting with certified English-fluent tutors living in countries with favorable exchange rates, your tuition dollars go much further. Our tutors receive competitive, highly attractive pay in their local currency while you save up to 75% on tuition fees.
