Our Story

Bridging the educational gap in Singapore through accessible, cross-border 1-on-1 tuition.

The Problem We Are Solving

Private tuition costs in Singapore have escalated into a major financial hurdle for lower- and middle-income households, widening the educational achievement gap. Local physical tuition rates for Secondary O and N-Level subjects frequently exceed SGD $50 to $80 per hour.

Our team recognized that highly qualified, English-fluent educators based in neighboring countries with favorable exchange rates (such as Malaysia, the Philippines, and Indonesia) can teach the exact same Singapore Cambridge curriculum over Zoom at a fraction of local rates — starting at just SGD $15 to $22 per hour. TutorBridge was built to connect these dedicated tutors directly with Singapore students in need.
