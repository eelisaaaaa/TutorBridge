Our Story

Why we built TutorBridge — solving Singapore's tuition affordability gap through global exchange rates.

The Problem: Tuition Cost vs. Exchange-Rate Advantage

Private tuition costs in Singapore are a significant financial barrier for lower- and middle-income families, widening the education gap between income groups. Local physical tuition rates average S$50–S$80 per hour per subject.

We realized that highly qualified, English-fluent educators based in countries with favorable exchange rates (such as South Africa, Malaysia, India, and the Philippines) can teach the exact same Cambridge GCE O-Level and N-Level syllabi over Zoom at a fraction of local costs — starting from just S$12–S$18 per hour.

Our Mission Statement

To provide Sec 1–5 students in Singapore with equal access to world-class, affordable subject tutoring regardless of their family's postcode or income level.

The Borderless Advantage

  • 75% Direct Savings: Pay S$14/hr instead of S$60/hr while receiving identical quality step-by-step guidance.
  • Rigorous Safety & Vetting: Every tutor undergoes identity verification, academic transcript checks, and mock lesson evaluations.
  • 1-on-1 Personalized Attention: Customized homework review, past-year paper drills, and exam technique coaching.
